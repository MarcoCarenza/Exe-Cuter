You’re a 5-year old girl, breaking into your dad’s office and making his special tools pretty as a surprise. To follow the theme, you are putting cute things, like stickers and toys, onto something that isn’t cute, a weapon.

_This game was created for the 2025 Games Now! Game Jam - with a theme of 'cute.'_
